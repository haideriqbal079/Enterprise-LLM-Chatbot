# Artificial Intelligence Capabilities

## Overview

Try Soft AI provides artificial intelligence capabilities focused on integrating intelligent systems into software products and business workflows.

## AI Development

The company describes capabilities for developing and integrating AI-powered applications.

AI can be incorporated into new applications or existing software systems depending on project requirements.

## Conversational AI

Conversational AI can enable software systems to interact with users through natural language.

Potential applications include:

- AI assistants
- Customer support
- Internal knowledge assistants
- Information systems
- Automated user interactions

## AI Agents

Try Soft AI describes AI agents and agent-powered workflows as part of its AI capabilities.

AI agents can be designed to:

- Understand user instructions
- Perform multi-step tasks
- Interact with software systems
- Use available tools
- Automate workflows
- Produce task results

## AI Automation

AI automation can combine artificial intelligence with business workflows to reduce repetitive manual work.

Potential applications include:

- Workflow automation
- Information processing
- Task automation
- Intelligent decision support
- Business process assistance

## Large Language Models

Large language models can be integrated into applications that require natural-language understanding and generation.

Potential applications include:

- Conversational assistants
- Knowledge assistants
- Content generation
- Natural-language interfaces
- Intelligent automation

## Machine Learning

Machine learning can be used to develop systems that learn patterns from data and produce predictions, classifications, or other analytical outputs.

## Vector Search

Vector search is among the publicly referenced technologies associated with the company's AI capabilities.

Vector search can be used to find semantically related information within suitable data collections.

## Custom AI Integrations

AI systems can be integrated with existing software, APIs, business systems, and workflows.

## Human-Centered AI

The company's public positioning emphasizes human-centered AI.

This means AI systems should be designed around real user needs and practical business problems rather than technology alone.

## Important Knowledge Boundary

The capabilities listed here describe technologies and services publicly associated with Try Soft AI.

They do not mean that every capability is included in every project or that every technology is currently deployed in every company system.
