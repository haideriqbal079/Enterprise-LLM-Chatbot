# Try Soft AI — Values

## Innovation

Try Soft AI emphasizes the use of emerging technologies and modern approaches to solve business and technical problems.

The company's public material highlights artificial intelligence, automation, modern software engineering, and cloud technologies.

## Purpose-Driven Technology

The company focuses on applying technology to practical business problems.

Solutions are intended to create useful outcomes rather than using technology simply for its own sake.

## Collaboration

Try Soft AI emphasizes collaboration between technical teams, clients, designers, and business stakeholders.

Understanding requirements and working closely with stakeholders is part of its described approach to solution development.

## Quality

The company emphasizes quality throughout the development lifecycle, including design, development, testing, deployment, security, and optimization.

## Human-Centered AI

The company presents its AI approach as human-centered, with an emphasis on creating AI systems that are useful, practical, and aligned with real-world human and business needs.
