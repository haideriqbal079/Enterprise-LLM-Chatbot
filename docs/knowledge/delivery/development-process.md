# Development Process

## Overview

Try Soft AI describes a development approach that combines understanding business requirements, designing solutions, developing software, testing, deployment, and ongoing improvement.

## 1. Requirement Understanding

The development process begins with understanding the client's business requirements, goals, challenges, and expected outcomes.

The purpose is to establish what problem the technology solution needs to solve.

## 2. Solution Planning

After understanding requirements, the appropriate technical approach and solution architecture can be planned.

The solution should consider factors such as:

- Functional requirements
- Technical requirements
- Scalability
- Security
- Integration requirements
- Deployment environment

## 3. Design

The solution is designed before and during implementation.

Design considerations can include:

- User experience
- User interface
- Software architecture
- Data flow
- System integrations
- Technical components

## 4. Development

Software components are implemented according to the planned requirements and architecture.

Development may involve web technologies, mobile technologies, backend systems, artificial intelligence, data systems, and cloud technologies depending on the project.

## 5. Testing and Quality Assurance

Testing and quality assurance are part of the development lifecycle.

Testing helps identify problems and verify that the developed solution meets its intended requirements.

## 6. Deployment

After development and testing, the solution can be deployed to its intended environment.

Cloud platforms and modern deployment technologies can be used depending on project requirements.

## 7. Monitoring and Optimization

After deployment, systems can be monitored and optimized.

Monitoring and optimization can help identify:

- Performance issues
- Reliability problems
- Operational issues
- Opportunities for improvement

## 8. Ongoing Support

Technology solutions may require continued maintenance, updates, optimization, and technical support after deployment.

## Important Knowledge Boundary

This document describes the publicly available development approach of Try Soft AI.

It should not be interpreted as a complete confidential internal engineering procedure or as a mandatory process followed identically by every project.
